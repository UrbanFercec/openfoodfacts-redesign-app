What this prototype cannot reach yet

  • a user list with products in it, and a row mid-swipe - never captured, batch 4
  • the create / rename / clear-confirm dialogs on a list - shipped, captured, not drawn
  • the way IN to the states - a search result that points at a deleted product, a search that failed, and a product tile that did not load all lead to 18-states and none is wired: the edits are on 05-search and 01-product-page, both published
  • the way IN to Compare - the scan header's `Compare 2 Products` (07-scan-states) and `Compare selected products` in a list's actions sheet (08-lists) both point at family 11 and are not wired yet
  • their third way in - the `Mode comparaison` scanner with a side rail (their own Comparaison_WIP artboards) - drawn on no screen of ours
  • the way IN to reporting - the product page's overflow sheet has three rows and `Report a problem` is not one of them (01-product-page, published)
  • the way IN to reporting a PHOTO - the photo screen has no report affordance at all, and neither does the shipped app (13-product-edit, published)
  • the other two products on Home - only Nutella has a product page drawn
  • the Contribute pane on a product, family 1
  • the frozen first intro screen - kept exactly as it ships by Urban's 2026-08-23 ruling, so it is deliberately not drawn and there is nothing to link to
  • the country picker itself - the onboarding country row opens it and it is marked as not drawn
  • every bottom sheet behind a 'learn' link - design-system.md §3h, ruled 2026-08-21
  • the six rows on Profile that lead into the settings tree - drawn, not yet wired: two of them wait on the About + Legal merge ruling
  • Open Food Facts Labs - ruled KEEP-undrawn, a dev-flag surface not a destination
  • the Prices tree - 15 shipped screens, family L, reached from My statistics
  • Licenses - Flutter's stock generated page, deliberately not redrawn
  • the language / country / currency pickers - the Theme sheet is the shape for all four
  • the four Contribute bottom sheets, and the four shipped dialogs
  • the filtered contribution list behind My statistics - family 8
  • the crop / rotate screen - ruled KEEP-undrawn: it is a gesture surface, and a static mock cannot say anything true about pinch, rotate and a minimum-pixel guard
  • 'Select from the product photos' - the uploaded-image picker, reached only from the replace-photo sheet, ruled KEEP-undrawn
  • the three edit sheets - the photo source picker, replace photo, and the 100+ language picker. All ruled SHEET rather than page; design-system.md §3i fixes their shape
  • 'Add a nutrient' - 55 server-supplied rows behind a search field, deliberately undrawn
  • the product FOOTER is still the missing route in: in the shipped app you reach edit from it, and we draw no footer (family 1). Same gap 08-lists names
  • the way IN to Guides - Home's `Guides` module draws two cards that lead nowhere (03-home is not this batch's file), and the FAQ screen's five `Discover ...` rows are drawn on 09-settings/about.html with no destination at all. See 12-guides decision C
  • the other seven guides' bodies - one reader, eight bodies, and only Green-Score is drawn
